# Stock material notes: making the parts from standard timber

Notes by Tony Hine on the v3 plans, tidied up from a voice recording made while going through the parts sheets. Part codes, quantities and sizes are checked against the v3 drawings ([PDF](../wooden-dump-truck-plans.pdf), sheets 2 to 10). Where a figure in the recording differed from the drawing, the drawing figure is used and the difference is noted.

## The idea

Many of the parts don't need to be exactly the thickness shown. Take the chassis rails (A1): they're drawn 18 mm thick, but in theory anything from about 18 mm to 50 mm would do. If you allow that kind of range, you can use far more of the standard timber sold at any merchant, and cut much less from plywood sheet.

A note on inch sizes: a "4 x 1" is the **sawn** size (about 100 x 25 mm). Once it has been planed (PAR, planed all round, or PSE, planed square edge) it comes out at about **94 x 18 mm**. So 18 mm is really nearer 3/4 in than 1 in. The table gives both the nominal name and the actual size. Actual sizes vary by a millimetre or two between merchants (some 4 x 1 is 20 or 21 mm thick), so measure your timber before cutting.

### Three rules for changing a size

1. **If a part fits between two others, change its length to match.** For example, the frame is 200 mm across the outside of the rails, so the cross members (A2) are 200 minus twice the rail thickness: 164 mm with 18 mm rails, 100 mm with 50 mm rails. Keep that 200 mm outside width, because the hinge posts and wheels sit outside the rails and the rear inner tyre clears the hinge post by only 11.5 mm. The same goes for panels: swap 12 mm ply for 18 mm timber and any panel fitted between the sides gets 12 mm shorter.
2. **Don't let the deck or cab get taller.** With the body down, there's only 10 mm between the body and the deck, the cab sides and the cab roof. The engine box panels (B1 to B3) set the deck height, and the rest blocks (A6) set the body height. Keep those to the drawing.
3. **The rail depth matters more than its thickness.** The axle holes are centred 47 mm down the 94 mm rail, and the cross members, axle blocks and hinge posts all match that 94.

The wheels themselves (W, six 330 mm puncture-proof wheels) are bought, not cut.

## Parts that must be cut from ply sheet

- **E1** body sides (wedge shape, 916 long)
- **E3** body floor panel (630 x 773.6)
- **E4** tail lip
- **H3 / H4** spacer washers, the 60 mm discs (or any flat offcut of the right thickness)
- **C1** cab sides, possibly (8x1 trimmed to 189 might work)
- **B7** deck, unless you have a wide pine board
